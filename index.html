Artistry rooted in tradition,

Founded by Punitha Paulraj MUA, Adhvi Bridal Studio is a sanctuary for brides across Thanjavur and Tamil Nadu who seek to look unmistakably themselves — only more luminous and timeless.

Located conveniently near Kulanthai Yesu Church Bus Stand on Pudukkottai Road, Thanjavur, our studio combines traditional South Indian bridal techniques with advanced HD airbrush technology. Every makeover is designed to photograph flawlessly under 4K video lights while maintaining a featherlight, comfortable feel all day.

  • Skin-first prep using international dermat-tested brands
  • Sweat-proof, tear-proof 18-hour airbrush formulation
  • Saree draping, pleating & jewellery styling assistance
  • Outstation wedding travel available on request
